UNDERSTAND THE MARKET

Why do stock prices change?

A stock price changes when buyers and sellers trade shares. Demand, company performance and market conditions can influence its price.

Why can prices rise? 📈

More people may want to buy a company's shares. Positive company results or encouraging news may increase demand.

Why can prices fall? 📉

More people may want to sell shares. Poor company results or negative news may reduce demand.

Remember this 💡

Stock prices can change for many reasons. A price increase does not guarantee future profits, and a decrease does not guarantee future losses.

STOCK MARKET FOR BEGINNERS

Learn the basics

Start with these simple concepts before exploring advanced analysis.

What is a share?

A share represents a small ownership unit in a company.

What is NSE?

The National Stock Exchange is a marketplace where securities are traded.

What is a bull market?

A market period in which prices are generally rising.

What is a bear market?

A market period in which prices are generally falling.

What is a dividend?

A portion of company profits distributed to eligible shareholders.

What is risk?

The possibility of losing some or all of the money invested.
